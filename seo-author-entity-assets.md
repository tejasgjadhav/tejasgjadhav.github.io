## 2. Goodreads Author Program — setup checklist

1. Sign in to Goodreads with the account you'll use as the author identity.
2. Search Goodreads for one of your book titles (e.g. *Claude AI for Finance
   Professionals*). Open the book's page → scroll to the author name → click it.
3. On the author page, scroll to **"Is this you? Let us know!"** → click it to apply
   for the **Goodreads Author Program** (it's free; approval is usually 1–3 days).
4. Once approved, set on the author profile:
   - **Photo:** upload the same blue-blazer portrait.
   - **Bio:** paste the **Long** bio above verbatim.
   - **Website:** `https://tejasgjadhav.github.io/`
   - Confirm all your titles are listed under the profile (combine duplicate editions).
5. After the author page has a stable URL (e.g. `goodreads.com/author/show/<id>.Tejas_Jadhav`),
   send it to me → I'll add **Goodreads author ID (P2963)** to Wikidata and the URL to the
   `sameAs` array on all three sites.
