**"Claude AI for Absolute Beginners"** lives at `~/files/kdp-books/claude-beginners`. It is

[[kdp-books]] follows.

## Where it stands (end of 2026-08-13)

The full draft is written. It runs to 75 chapters across 15 parts plus seven back-matter sections,
243 pages, with both bindings passing and the EPUB built. Sixteen drawn figures and 13 real product
screenshots are wired into the pages. The de-fluff pass at the corrected register is complete for
Chapters 1 to 17.

Remaining work, in order: the de-fluff pass over Parts 4 to 15, four outstanding figures (three of
them need the Cowork desktop app and one context-warning shot is optional), the preview-zone

## Rules specific to this book

- The byline is **TEJAS JADHAV** alone, with no CFA and no FRM. He asked for this directly, and it
  overrides the usual name rule.
- The "AI FOR FINANCE" series mark does not apply here.
- The reading level targets a bright 12-to-15-year-old, with sentences of 10 to 18 words and common
  words carrying adult content. That agrees with [[voice-pass]] rather than fighting it.
- Every chapter title carries a verb. He rejected a noun-phrase title as a voice-pass failure.
- **The register rule, as he corrected it:** no fluff is universal, so no anecdotal openers and
  every paragraph pays the reader. Pointwise is conditional, so numbered lists appear only where
  the content genuinely enumerates and explanatory prose stays prose. He named the offending
  sentence himself, an opener about paying for his first month on a Tuesday, and it is gone.

## Two things the screenshot pass proved

- **The product moved under the prose.** There is no separate tools button anymore, because tools
  now live in the "+" menu; extended thinking is the effort setting beside the model picker; and
  artifacts render inline at narrow widths. Chapters 6, 9 and 10 were rewritten against the real
  screens. A book about a weekly-shipping product goes stale in its sentences, not only in its
  pictures, so re-check the screens before every edition.
- **Images from the finance books mostly cannot be reused, and the reason is content rather than
  quality.** An audit of about 58 images across the three finance titles found five reusable, and
  all five were already in. The rest carry client and desk material baked into the pixels, such as
  a session list reading "UBS corporate actions" or rupee P&L inside a screenshot.

Interior identity is deliberately unlike the finance series: Palatino and Avenir Next with deep
teal, clay and brick, so a reader who owns two of his books does not see the same styling twice.

## The cover, built from his own artwork (2026-08-16)

He supplied a generated front cover rather than running the pipeline from scratch, and three
things had to change. The word BIBLE came out, because it was the most competitor-specific
element the file carried, and the freed band was absorbed into the title so it did not read as a
hole. A small fan-shaped ornament above his byline read as a miniature Anthropic starburst and
was removed. The halftone was rebuilt as the graded field his reference actually shows, with a
run down the full left edge. The reasoning behind all three, and the finding that the real risk
is an Amazon rights complaint rather than a lawsuit, sits in [[book-covers]].

His source file was 1023 px wide, which is a print problem and not a Kindle one: the 1.83× blowup
a 6×9 bleed needs lands at an effective 164 DPI. A first wrap build also mirrored the front
artwork under the back panel and printed the description over it, which every geometry gate
passed. A true 1600 × 2560 Kindle front now exists, replacing the 1:1.5 file built earlier.

The listing package carries three bindings, each traced to a past failure: every claim verified
against the manuscript first, keywords probed against Amazon's own autocomplete rather than
guessed, and **no CFA/FRM in the bio on this title**, because it is a general-audience beginner
book and the finance credentials belong on the finance books. See [[kdp-listing-operations]] and
[[tejas-jadhav]].

The book now points readers at his personal site rather than the BSR dashboard, and the Anthropic
relationship is stated as a trademark notice with the book positioned as an independent education
guide.
