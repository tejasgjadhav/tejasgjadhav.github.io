[[tejas-jadhav]] asked on 2026-08-13 whether **"Claude AI for Your Dream Job"** would sell. The
answer was no, not as a lead title. It was reached by pulling live ranks with his own
[[kdp-dashboard]] scraper rather than by reasoning from memory, which is the standing rule whenever
the subject is a Claude book.

## What the shelf showed

| Book | Age | Rank | Reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Claude AI for Job Seekers* (Quinn Alder) | 12 days | Kindle #1,397,655 · #143 in Job Resumes | 0 |
| *Career Coach GPT* (the category leader) | 3 years | Kindle #149,573 · #6 in Job Resumes | 710 |
| *Land the Job with AI* | 4 months | #625 in Job Resumes | 1 |

## Three reasons it was rejected

1. **The exact idea already shipped and it is not selling.** Someone published *Claude AI for Job
   Seekers* on 1 August 2026 with the same concept, an ATS-beating CV plus interview prep plus 50
   prompts, and at twelve days old it sat at 1.4 million with zero reviews. The Claude-branded
   job-search buyer barely exists yet.
2. **The ceiling of the whole niche is low.** The best AI job-search book on Amazon sells roughly
   two or three copies a day after three years and 710 reviews. That is the prize for winning the
   category outright, and his finance titles in [[kdp-books]] already have a higher ceiling than
   this niche's number one.
3. **"Dream Job" is a self-help phrase rather than a search term.** Buyers type "resume", "ATS",
   "job search" and "interview", so the title as phrased would rank for nothing.

## The one honest angle

Every book on that shelf is a prompt list. He is actually running a Claude-automated job hunt, with
CV alignment against real job descriptions, ATS sweeps, tracked applications and cover-letter
pipelines. A book built from those real artifacts would be genuinely different from everything on
the shelf. Treat it as a cheap spoke assembled from material that already exists, and budget the
effort accordingly. It is not the next lead title, and it is not the fix for the August sales dip.
That fix is rank recovery on the flagship, which is set out in [[amazon-ads]].
